Cookie Information

Understanding Cookies on This Website

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites and online services may store in a visitor's browser to support functionality, remember settings, or help services operate correctly.

This academic portfolio does not intentionally create custom tracking cookies through its own HTML code. However, some third-party resources and embedded services used by the site may apply their own cookies or similar browser technologies.

This Cookie Policy explains those possibilities and helps distinguish the website's own front-end code from services that may operate independently.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or pieces of browser data that may be stored on a device when a website or online service is used. They can help websites remember preferences, maintain sessions, or provide certain technical functions.

Different websites use cookies for different purposes. Some are necessary for functionality, while others may support analytics, personalization, advertising, or other services.

2. Cookies Used by This Website

This academic portfolio does not intentionally create its own custom advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies through the HTML and CSS developed for the project.

The primary purpose of the site is to demonstrate front-end web development, responsive design, forms, navigation, portfolio content, and embedded services.

3. Third-Party Services

The website uses or references third-party technologies such as Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, content delivery networks, and Google Maps.

These services operate independently and may use cookies, browser storage, request logs, or other technologies according to their own technical and privacy practices.

4. Google Maps

The Contact page includes an embedded Google Map. When an embedded map is loaded, the external map service may communicate with its own servers and may use cookies or related browser technologies.

Any such activity is controlled by the external service rather than by the custom HTML and CSS code of this portfolio website.

5. Content Delivery Networks

Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons are loaded from external content delivery networks. These services may receive technical requests from a visitor's browser when the website loads.

The handling of those requests is governed by the respective third-party service providers and their own policies.

6. Browser Storage

Modern browsers support several types of local storage, including cookies, local storage, session storage, and cached files.

This academic portfolio does not intentionally use custom JavaScript to store visitor account information, passwords, or form submissions in browser storage.

7. Sign In and Sign Up Pages

The Sign In and Sign Up pages are front-end demonstrations only. They do not create real user sessions or authentication cookies.

No production login system, session-management service, or account database is connected to these forms.

8. Contact Form

The Contact form is also a front-end demonstration. It does not intentionally store form submissions in cookies or browser storage.

Its purpose is to demonstrate the structure and design of a contact form within a responsive website.

9. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow users to review, block, delete, or limit cookies through browser settings. The exact options depend on the browser and device being used.

  • Visitors can review stored cookies through browser settings.
  • Cookies can usually be deleted manually.
  • Browsers may allow third-party cookies to be blocked.
  • Some embedded services may not function normally if certain cookies or browser features are disabled.

10. Essential and Non-Essential Cookies

A production website may use essential cookies for login sessions, security, preferences, or other required functionality. It may also use optional cookies for analytics or personalization.

This portfolio does not operate a production account system, so it does not intentionally create essential login-session cookies through its own code.

11. Privacy and Cookies

Cookies and privacy are closely related because some cookies can store identifiers or information about browser activity.

Visitors can review the separate Privacy Policy for more information about how this academic project describes form data, external services, and information handling.

12. Changes to This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated if the website changes, new services are added, or additional clarification is needed.

Updated information may be published on this page so visitors can review the current version of the policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this Cookie Policy or the website may be directed through the Contact page.

The Contact page includes a demonstration form and embedded map as part of the academic website project.
