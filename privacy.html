Privacy Policy

Privacy Information

Understanding Privacy on This Website

This website was created as an academic portfolio project and includes several common website features such as contact forms, sign-in and sign-up interfaces, navigation links, embedded content, and responsive page layouts.

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to explain how those features relate to information handling and privacy. Because this site is a front-end demonstration, most interactive features do not send information to a live server or database.

Visitors should still understand that external services, browsers, hosting platforms, or embedded content may operate independently and may apply their own privacy practices.

1. Purpose of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the general privacy approach of this academic portfolio website and explains how information entered into demonstration forms is treated within the scope of the project.

The website is intended primarily for educational, informational, and portfolio-presentation purposes rather than commercial data collection.

2. Information Entered Into Forms

The website includes Contact, Sign In, and Sign Up forms. These forms are included to demonstrate HTML form controls, Bootstrap styling, responsive layout, and user-interface design.

The forms are not connected to a production database, account system, or email-delivery service. Information entered into these forms is therefore not intentionally stored by this website.

3. Contact Form Information

The Contact page contains fields such as name, email address, subject, reason for contact, and message. These fields demonstrate how a structured contact interface can be designed.

Submitting the form does not guarantee delivery of a message because no live form-processing service is connected to the website.

4. Sign In and Sign Up Information

The Sign In and Sign Up pages are front-end demonstrations only. They do not create user accounts, authenticate users, or store passwords in a live account system.

Visitors should not enter real passwords or sensitive personal information into demonstration forms on an academic website.

5. Personal Information

This website does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through a production data-processing system.

Portfolio content may include general academic and professional information about the website creator for the purpose of presenting technical interests and experience.

6. Google Maps and Embedded Content

The Contact page includes an embedded Google Map. Embedded services may communicate directly with their own servers and may process information according to their own policies.

Visitors should review the privacy practices of external service providers if they want additional information about how those services handle data.

7. Third-Party Resources

This website uses third-party resources such as Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, Google Maps, and externally hosted content delivery networks.

These third-party services operate independently from this academic portfolio and may apply their own technical, privacy, or logging practices.

8. Browser and Technical Information

Browsers and hosting services may automatically process limited technical information when a website is visited. This can include information such as browser type, device characteristics, general network information, and request details.

This academic website does not independently analyze or maintain a visitor-tracking database for such information.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website does not intentionally set custom tracking cookies through its own HTML code. However, embedded or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Additional information is available on the separate Cookie Policy page.

10. Data Security

Because this is a front-end academic project, it does not operate a production database containing user accounts or submitted form data.

A real production website would require additional safeguards such as encrypted connections, secure password storage, server-side validation, access controls, and secure data-management practices.

11. External Links

This website may contain links to other pages, tools, or services. Once visitors follow an external link, the privacy practices of that external service may apply.

This academic portfolio is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website changes, additional features are introduced, or clarification is needed.

Updated information may be published on this page so visitors can review the current privacy description.

13. Contact

Questions about this website or this Privacy Policy may be directed through the Contact page.

The Contact page itself is also part of the academic demonstration and does not currently use a live message-delivery system.
